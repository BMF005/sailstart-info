SailStart Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

SailStart is an Apple Watch sailing timer developed by Christopher Johnson.

Information handled by the app

SailStart does not require an account and does not transmit your timer or workout information to the developer. The app contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics.

SailStart stores timer settings, the current race timer, workout preferences, and information needed to recover or save an unfinished workout locally on your device.

Apple Health and workout recording

Workout recording is optional. With your permission, SailStart uses Apple HealthKit to save sailing workouts, including duration, start and end times, and associated race timing information, to your Health store. An active sailing workout also supports wrist-down countdown haptics.

SailStart does not request access to read your existing health history or record GPS routes or heart-rate measurements.

SailStart does not sell health or fitness data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for advertising, marketing, or data mining.

Apple manages Health data storage, syncing, backups, and sharing according to your settings. Your workout data may sync between your devices through Apple’s services. See Apple’s Health privacy information.

Your choices and data deletion

You can use the timer without enabling workout recording. You can turn recording off in SailStart’s Sailing settings and revoke its permission to write workouts through the Health app.

Reset clears the current race timer and discards an active, unsaved workout. Completed workouts already saved to Health remain there until you delete them through Apple’s Health or Fitness tools. Unsaved workouts awaiting a save retry can be discarded from SailStart’s Sailing settings.

App settings remain on your device until changed or the app and its local data are deleted. The developer does not hold a server-side copy of your timer or workout information and cannot access or delete records in your Health store.

Support correspondence

If you email support, Christopher Johnson receives your email address and any information you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Correspondence is retained as needed for those purposes; you can request its deletion by email. Please avoid sending sensitive health information.

This website

These information pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, under its Privacy Statement. This is separate from information handled by the SailStart app.
