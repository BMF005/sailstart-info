SailStart Support

SailStart is a regatta countdown and race timer for Apple Watch, developed by Christopher Johnson.

For questions, feedback, or help, email SailStart@proton.me.

When reporting a problem, please include your Apple Watch model, watchOS version, and a brief description of what happened. Please do not include sensitive health information.

Timer controls

  • Turn the Digital Crown to select a starting sequence from 1–30 minutes.
  • Tap Mark to start the selected countdown.
  • During the countdown, turn the Digital Crown to adjust the selected sequence, then tap Mark to restart the countdown using that duration. Turning the Crown alone does not change the running countdown.
  • After the countdown reaches zero, the timer displays elapsed race time.
  • Tap Stop to hold the finish time and save an enabled sailing workout.
  • Use the orange header Reset at any time to return to setup. This discards an active workout without saving it. After Stop, the main button also becomes Reset.

Workout recording and wrist-down alerts

Open the sailing icon to enable optional workout recording or choose Use timer only.

Wrist-down countdown haptics require an active SailStart sailing workout and permission to save workouts to Health. In timer-only mode, countdown haptics require the app to be active.

Only one workout session can run at a time. Starting another workout can interrupt SailStart’s recording. Watch settings and system conditions can affect haptics and display updates.

Health permissions

Manage SailStart’s workout-writing permission in the Health app on your iPhone. Turning off workout recording does not delete workouts previously saved to Health.
